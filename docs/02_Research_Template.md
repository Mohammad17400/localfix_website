# Requirement gathering — interview template

No interviews have been conducted or invented in this package. Record real findings here.

## Customers

1. How do you currently find a plumber, electrician, cleaner or appliance technician?
2. What information helps you choose a provider?
3. What difficulties have you encountered?
4. How do you arrange and confirm a visit?
5. What would you want to see after submitting a request?

## Providers

1. How do customers currently contact you?
2. What information do you need before accepting work?
3. How do you organize requests and appointments?
4. How do you explain your prices and service areas?
5. Which parts of a simple online request system would be most useful?
